Check before you trust.

URLume helps explain warning signs in links, QR codes, and files you deliberately select. This page provides support, privacy details, and an honest description of what the app can—and cannot—do.

Privacy-first by design

No account registration. Selected files and inspection history stay on your device. Version 1.1 includes ads in the free tier; Premium removes them.

Support

For help with inspections, Premium access, purchases, or unexpected behavior, contact the developer. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a description of what happened. Do not email confidential files or passwords.

Common steps

  • Use Settings → Restore Purchases after reinstalling or changing iPhones.
  • Enable camera permission in iOS Settings to scan QR codes.
  • Files stored in iCloud may need to download before inspection begins.
  • Online reputation checks can be disabled at any time in URLume Settings.

Important limitations

URLume is an inspector, not system-wide antivirus software. It cannot scan iOS, monitor other apps, remove malware, or certify that a link or file is safe.

Its findings are based on observable structure, selected document markers, file signatures, and optional hostname reputation information. New, targeted, encrypted, or sophisticated threats may not contain those indicators.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Version 1.1. Earlier version 1.0 builds do not include advertising.

Information collected by the developer

The developer does not operate an account system or receive your inspected links, selected files, or inspection history. Those inputs are not sent to the advertising service. Third-party services process limited information as described below; non-personalized advertising does not mean no data collection.

Advertising and privacy choices in version 1.1

The free version uses Google AdMob for bottom banners and occasional full-screen ads at navigation breaks. Premium removes both formats. Google may process IP address and approximate location derived from it, device/app identifiers, advertisements viewed and ad interactions, crash reports, and performance/diagnostic data to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud, and improve its services. Requests are non-personalized, publisher first-party identifiers are disabled, and URLume does not request access to Apple's advertising identifier or precise location. No inspected URL, selected file, fingerprint, or inspection history is provided as ad-targeting data.

Google's User Messaging Platform presents applicable ad consent choices before requesting ads. Where available, use Settings → Ad Privacy Choices to review or withdraw consent. Ads may be unavailable if consent or a connection is unavailable; inspections still work within the free allowance. Google controls retention of information it receives; review Google's advertising privacy information and Google's privacy policy for its purposes, retention, and controls.

Use Report an inappropriate ad in Settings, or Report ad below a banner, to contact support. The prepared email includes an ad response identifier if available and is sent only if you choose to send it.

Selected files and local history

URLume receives temporary access only to a file you select with Apple’s document picker. The app reads that file to calculate a SHA-256 fingerprint and inspect readable structure. It does not upload or permanently copy the selected file. Inspection results—including the filename, size, reported and detected type, fingerprint, and findings—remain in the app’s private local storage until you delete them or remove the app.

Camera

Camera access is requested only when you open the QR scanner. Video frames are processed live on the device to recognize QR-code text and are not recorded, retained, or uploaded by URLume.

Links, Quad9, and consent

Link structure is inspected locally without visiting the destination. If you enable Online Reputation Checks, URLume sends only the hostname—not its path, query, fragment, or a selected file—to Quad9 using encrypted DNS-over-HTTPS. Quad9 returns whether its threat intelligence blocks that hostname. You can decline this feature or disable it later in URLume Settings.

Quad9 states that it does not record end-user IP addresses or maintain user accounts, and that it does not share, sell, or rent information that could identify an individual user. URLume relies on those protections for the limited hostname query. Review Quad9’s privacy policy for its retention, deletion, and contact practices.

If you deliberately choose Reveal Redirects, URLume sends a lightweight request directly to the address you supplied so the destination server can reveal its final URL. The developer does not receive or retain that request; the destination website and network provider may process connection information under their own policies.

Purchases

The optional URLume Premium monthly subscription is purchased and managed through Apple. URLume receives only a signed entitlement indicating whether Premium is active and does not receive or store payment-card information. Premium provides unlimited inspections and removes ads while active. Subscription cancellation and billing are managed through the user’s Apple Account. The subscription uses Apple's Standard Terms of Use (EULA).

Retention, deletion, and choices

Delete individual inspection results by swiping them in History, or use Clear to remove all saved results. Removing URLume removes its local app data. Disable Online Reputation Checks in Settings to stop future hostname queries. Because the developer does not operate an account system or receive app data, the developer has no server-side personal profile to retrieve or delete.

Children and policy changes

URLume is a general-audience utility and is not designed specifically for children. It requests general-audience ad content. The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information; third-party advertising practices are described above. Material changes to this policy will be published on this page and reflected in an app update when appropriate.
